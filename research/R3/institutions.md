# R3 — Descriptive institutional timeline
The event date and outcome measurement date are separate. Legal dates do not establish effects on wages, labor shares, capital gains, union membership or corporate payout policy.

| Date | Source-grounded event | Scope and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| 1935 | National Labor Relations Act established NLRB and collective-bargaining framework. [NLRB](https://www.nlrb.gov/about-nlrb/who-we-are/our-history) | Some groups of workers excluded; not a union density measure. |
| 1947 | Taft–Hartley amendments altered NLRA rules; state right-to-work provisions permitted. [NLRB](https://www.nlrb.gov/about-nlrb/who-we-are/our-history) | State adoption dates/coverage vary; avoid one national treatment coefficient. |
| 1959 | Landrum–Griffin labor management reporting and disclosure law. [DOL](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/olms/laws) | Reporting/governance not equivalent to collective bargaining prevalence. |
| 1979 | Starting year of CBO currently published distributional household-income historic series. [CBO](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/62792) | Statistical-series beginning, not economic breakpoint. |
| 1981–1988 | Top federal individual statutory income rate reductions: top ordinary rate 70% before 1982, 50% in 1982 and 28% in 1988, with transitional schedules. [IRS historical SOI](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/indincdi.pdf) | Rates apply above taxable brackets; not actual average tax paid. |
| 1983 | Comparable CPS/BLS union member series begins at 20.1% of wage/salary employed. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/union2.htm) | Earlier historical union data require separate methodology. |
| 1986–1988 | Tax Reform Act of 1986 changed brackets, tax base and incentives for income reporting. [IRS SOI](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/indincdi.pdf) | Income shifting and tax-return coverage can affect measured AGI; no effect estimated. |
| 1993 | Federal top individual marginal ordinary rate 39.6%. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8621) | Tax schedule, not observed effective rate. |
| 2003 | Top ordinary rate 35%; 2013 restored 39.6% through 2017. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8621) | Thresholds and filing status matter. |
| 2010 | IRS SOI top percentile tabulation underwent population/positive-AGI definition revision. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/statistics/soi-tax-stats-individual-income-tax-rates-and-tax-shares) | Measurement break distinct from enacted statute. |
| 2015 | SEC Item 402(u) CEO/employee compensation ratio disclosure finalized; first covered fiscal years begin 2017. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2015/08/pay-ratio-disclosure) | Issuers differ in median methodology. |
| 2018 | Tax Cuts and Jobs Act corporate statutory income tax 21%, top individual 37% on applicable years. [IRS corporate guidance](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part21/irm_21-007-004r); [IRS schedules](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8621) | Corporate and personal effective taxes distinct; provisions expire on different schedules. |
| 2023 | Federal 1% excise on covered corporate stock repurchases generally effective for eligible transactions after Dec 31 2022. [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-provides-guidance-on-stock-repurchase-excise-tax) | Applies to excise-defined **net** repurchases and exceptions, not S&P gross-quarter benchmark. |
| 2025 | CPS union data exclude October due to federal government shutdown; switched to 2022 Census industry classification effective Jan2025 without revising historical industry tables. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/union2.htm) | Industry sector year-over-year comparisons have coverage/classification limits. |
| 2025 | BEA distributional personal-income experimental nowcast (2024 provisional). [BEA](https://www.bea.gov/data/special-topics/distribution-of-personal-income) | Methodological time point, not observed microdata from 2025. |

**Not yet documented sufficiently to code as exogenous policy changes:** state right-to-work law adoption/effective dates, buyback Rule 10b-18 safe harbor (1982) and modifications, agency enforcement capacity, sector collective-bargaining exceptions, antitrust and securities regulation, foreign shareholder taxation, estate and capital-gains schedules. These are candidate institutional covariates; do not describe them as causal explanations on the basis of this chronology alone.
