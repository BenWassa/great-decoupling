# R1 definitions and comparability matrix

Status: provisional research alternatives, **not** approval of decision #1 or #2. Dates/coverage refer to published source ranges observed on **2026-10-24**, and can change on revision.

| Item | Unit and population | Calculation/price concept | Coverage / use | Disallowed substitution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Labor productivity **OPHNFB** | Index of **real value-added output / hours of all persons**, U.S. nonfarm business (2017=100); not entire GDP | BLS major-sector output/hour; “gross” output concept versus net depreciation-adjusted productivity | Quarterly seasonally adjusted 1947 Q1–2026 Q2 at retrieval; R1 anchor benchmark | Not economy-wide average hourly output; not TFP; not an individual worker’s marginal product |
| Nominal hourly compensation **COMPNFB** | Aggregate **wages + supplements/benefits + imputed self-employment labor income**, divided by all-person nonfarm-business hours; weighted *mean* (2017=100) | Current-dollar numerator divided by hours; index | Quarterly SA from 1947 Q1; real deflators applied **after** ratio | Not take-home pay, taxable wages, median wage, or the average of individuals’ pay |
| Real hourly compensation **COMPRNFB** | Same mean nonfarm-business compensation coverage | BLS consumer-price-adjusted historical index (2017=100); BLS uses CPI-U-RS for earlier periods, recent CPI-U | Quarterly SA from 1947 Q1; **consumer purchasing-power lens** | Not necessarily COMP/CPIAUCSL ratio; not cash wages only |
| Real **product wage** | Same mean compensation | COMPNFB / **IPDNBS**, nonfarm-business value-added output-price index; rebase for trends | Quarterly from 1947; **producer-price lens** matched sector | Not consumer purchasing power; normalization of the price index required for levels |
| PCE-adjusted compensation | Same mean compensation | COMPNFB / **PCEPI** (BEA, chain-type PCE; household-consumption price basket); monthly index averaged over exact quarter | Monthly PCE starts **1959**, overlapping 1960–2025 for quarter averages | Not CPI-U-RS; do not splice pre-1959 with another PCE measure |
| CPI-U-adjusted compensation | Same mean compensation | COMPNFB / **CPIAUCSL**, monthly official seasonally adjusted CPI-U averaged within quarter | Monthly begins 1947; source has **missing Oct 2025** | Not same as published BLS COMPRNFB before present; missing data remain missing |
| R-CPI-U-RS | Research retroactive CPI-U time series | BLS applies most later improvements retroactively; separate versioned XLSX, source notices | Published from 1977/1978 to recent years; retrieval/version-sensitive | No interpolation to 1947; no assumption of error-free backcasting |
| Median usual **weekly** real earnings **LEU0252881600A** | CPS **full-time wage-and-salary workers, aged 16+**, median earnings per week; **1982–84 CPI-adjusted dollars**, not SA | Annual observation, 1979–2025 | Independent **typical full-time employee** proxy, not matched-hours hourly compensation | Cannot be relabelled “median hourly compensation”; excludes benefits, part time, most self-employment |
| Average hourly cash earnings **AHETPI** | CES **production and nonsupervisory employees** on private payrolls; mean hourly cash earnings | Nominal dollars/hour, SA monthly (starts 1964); deflate explicitly; annual mean monthly levels | Worker-coverage/wages-only sensitivity | Not median, all-worker mean, or benefits-inclusive |
| Average hourly earnings **CES0500000003** | CES **all employees** on private payrolls | Nominal cash/hour, SA monthly from 2006 | Modern wider-employee scope only | Cannot backcast to 1973 or equate to compensation |
| Employee benefits **ECEC** | Cross-sectional **employer cost / hour worked**, private or civilian workforce selected explicitly | Wages vs benefits vs total; current USD per hour, survey-dependent | June 2026 verified; **not** a coherent 1947–2025 time series | Not a historical benefits multiplier for CPS/CES wages |
| Employment Cost Index (ECI) | Fixed-employment-mix compensation cost index, employed civilians/private sector | Wages and benefits subseries; different weighting from changing-average-pay series | Post-1980 historical coverage depends on component | Not mean/median changes; not sectoral output deflator |
| Total factor productivity (TFP) | BLS private nonfarm-business **real output / weighted combined inputs of labor and capital** | TFP annual growth | 1987–2025 selected release | Not output/hour of labor or a pay comparator without conversion |
| **Net** productivity | Gross value added less depreciation / labor hours | NIPA consumption of fixed capital deduction (sector harmonization required) | **Not constructed** in this run | Not OPHNFB; don't label gross series “net” |
| OECD labour compensation per hour | Total employee compensation divided by employee hours, national-accounts definitions | Comparable OECD price index required, possibly producer-deflated | Contingent on decision #1 | Not identical to BLS all-person U.S. nonfarm-business measures |

**Denominator rules:** The main comparison shares **nonfarm-business hours** for productivity and mean compensation, but one is output per **all-person hour** and the other includes imputed proprietors' labor earnings. CPS median earnings refer to workers/weekly hours, not the matched sector denominator. Employment definitions and composition change.

**Growth vs level:** Since OPHNFB, COMPNFB, COMPRNFB and IPDNBS are index numbers on potentially different units, compare **percentage growth between the same two dates**, or rebase each series to 100 on that date. A difference of **percentage points of cumulative growth** is not dollars/hour or the fraction of GDP not received by workers.

**The “middle class” remains undefined.** A CPS earnings median, a statistical median hourly employee compensation, and a middle-income household category describe different populations and must not be collapsed. Household outcomes belong to R2 and are explicitly out of scope.
